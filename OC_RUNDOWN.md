# Tismtopia #arte: OC rundown

**Source:** `arte  TismTopia.txt`, a Discord #arte export with 4,358 messages, 2021-12-27 to 2026-11-22. Every line was read.

**How to read it**
- **OC** means a character a member made up: people, sonas and personas, creatures and species, and D&D/TTRPG characters. OCs based on a game or show are included, as you asked. Fan art of existing characters is left out (see the end).
- **Owner** is the Discord display name the chat credits. "(implied)" means the context points there but nobody says it outright.
- **Art refs** read `file name` · date · poster · **L** (line in the export). The export has file names only, not the images, so find each post on Discord by date and line. Generic names like `image.png` and `unknown.png` repeat across posts, so the line is the reliable part.
- **jegg also posts as "greef and eef".** Per your note they are one person, so they're merged under jegg.
- **Unsure** items are collected at the end, each with the question to answer.

## Owners at a glance
- **resident scrunge**: the most OCs. Deepwoken (a Roblox game) and Genshin-inspired characters, plus species and TTRPG characters.
- **jegg** (incl. greef and eef): personas, Wings of Fire OCs, Dandy's World toons, a robot OC.
- **the jangle jingler** (called "Jewels" in chat): personas, D&D characters, a gummy dragon.
- **Lord of Bad Timing**: D&D characters.
- **Jimmy Wrex**: Arhakoth and a D&D character.
- **Toasted Goose**: Pokémon-style fangame creatures and Minecraft mobs.
- **Deleted User**, **Coza**, **anonagon**: one OC each.

## Before this goes into a TCG
- **Owners:** chatting about an OC isn't a license. Get written permission from each creator.
- **Artists:** the art is by different people (scrunge, jegg, jangle, Toasted Goose and others). A commercial use needs the artist's permission, even if the OC owner agrees.
- **Base IP:** OCs based on other works still partly belong to someone else: Deepwoken (Roblox), Genshin, Zenless Zone Zero, Wings of Fire, Trigun, Transformers, Rain World, D&D/Eberron, Dandy's World (Roblox), and Touchstarved. The Pokémon-style fakemon creatures are the riskiest. A card game may need the base IP holder's OK, not just the artist's.
- **Files:** the export has names only, so you'll download each image from Discord yourself. Some may be unavailable if the original post was deleted.
